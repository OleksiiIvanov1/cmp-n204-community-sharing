# Community Share – Code of Conduct

## Purpose
All group members agree to follow the rules outlined below for the duration of the project to ensure fair contribution, clear communication, and a professional working environment.

---

## Commitment to Tasks
- Each member is responsible for completing the tasks assigned to them within the agreed timeframe.  
- Tasks will be assigned and tracked using the GitHub Project board.  
- All members are expected to actively contribute and make consistent progress.

---

## Communication
- Always communicate respectfully and professionally.  
- If a member anticipates difficulty completing a task on time, they must inform the group in advance and propose a revised plan or deadline.

---

## Accountability
To maintain fairness and transparency, the group adopts the following accountability process:

1. **First strike:** An assigned task is not completed without prior communication. The issue will be discussed within the group to clarify expectations and support completion.  
2. **Second strike:** Repeated failure to complete tasks or communicate responsibly. The issue will be recorded in the meeting notes and tasks may be reassigned if necessary.  
3. **Third strike:** Continued lack of contribution. The matter will be escalated to the module tutor, supported by evidence from GitHub commits, project boards, and meeting records.

---

## Fair Contribution
- All members are expected to contribute a reasonable and balanced share of work.  
- Consistently missing deadlines or relying on others to complete assigned tasks is not acceptable.

---

## Respect and Professional Behaviour
- All members must treat each other with respect.  
- Disrespectful behaviour, including dismissive communication, hostility, or undermining others, will not be tolerated.  
- Any disagreements should be addressed calmly and constructively.

---

## Escalation
- If issues cannot be resolved internally, the group reserves the right to escalate the matter to the module team in accordance with module procedures.
